# Source and Rights Policy

- Prefer an authoritative source record, official repository page, publisher landing page, DOI, or an organization-maintained protocol record.
- Record the exact URL used, source type, source record ID or DOI when available, access date, publication date, update date, and record-level reuse evidence.
- Treat discoverability, free reading, open access, and a site-wide license as separate from record-level reuse permission.
- Preserve only short factual evidence notes and links in this workspace by default. Do not copy instructions, tables, figures, videos, PDFs, or extended prose from a source.
- When a source is inaccessible, blocked, materially variant, duplicate, or license-unclear, retain a metadata-only screened entry with a reason instead of silently deleting it.
- Do not claim an author, date, DOI, license, source identifier, or protocol detail that is not verifiable from the source.
