# Heat-Shock Transformation of DH5α Competent E. coli

*Vendor insert (demo source). Hand this file to your agent and say: "Import this

## Materials
- DH5α chemically competent cells — 50 µL per reaction
- Plasmid DNA — 1–5 µL (≈10 pg to 100 ng)
- SOC outgrowth medium — room temperature
- LB agar plates with the appropriate selective antibiotic

## Procedure
1. Thaw competent cells on ice for about 10 minutes.
2. Add 1–5 µL plasmid DNA to 50 µL cells and flick gently to mix. Do not pipette up and down.
3. Incubate the mixture on ice for 30 minutes.
4. Heat shock at 42 °C for exactly 30 seconds. (Some cell lots specify 45 seconds — check the lot sheet.)
5. Place back on ice for 2 minutes.
6. Add 950 µL room-temperature SOC medium.
7. Shake at 37 °C, 250 rpm, for 60 minutes.
8. Spread onto selective LB agar and incubate overnight at 37 °C.
